# DisasterWatch BD

**DisasterWatch BD** is a disaster preparedness and emergency response platform built for Bangladesh.
It is designed to keep people informed, connected, and safe during floods, cyclones, earthquakes, and other emergencies 
even when internet access, mobile networks, or power infrastructure become unavailable.

The platform brings together live hazard monitoring, offline communication, AI-powered emergency support,
disaster news access, and critical location services into one resilient system.

---

## Why DisasterWatch BD Exists

During a disaster, the biggest problems are not always the disaster itself
it is the loss of communication, reliable information, and access to help.

DisasterWatch BD is built to reduce that gap by helping people:

- stay connected without internet
- access emergency guidance instantly
- find nearby shelters and hospitals
- track dangerous zones on a map
- receive important updates even offline
- avoid misinformation during crises

---

## Key Features

### Offline Mesh Network Communication

DisasterWatch BD includes a Bluetooth-based mesh communication system that allows nearby devices to connect directly
without internet access.

This means users can:

- send emergency messages locally
- share critical updates
- coordinate with nearby people
- stay connected when networks fail

Each device can act as a relay node, helping extend communication across affected areas.

---

### AI Emergency Assistant

The platform includes an AI-powered emergency assistant that provides support during crisis situations,
even with limited or no internet connectivity.

It can help with:

- first aid guidance
- emergency survival instructions
- evacuation advice
- disaster safety protocols
- medical emergency support
- quick decision-making during panic

---

### Live Disaster Monitoring Map

A real-time hazard map helps users understand which areas are currently affected.

Users can view:

- flood-affected zones
- cyclone-affected areas
- high-risk regions
- emergency alerts
- location-based danger zones

This makes complex disaster data easier to understand and act on.

---

### Smart Offline News System

Important disaster updates are automatically saved whenever internet access is available,
so users can still read them later without a connection.

This includes:

- emergency announcements
- government instructions
- weather alerts
- safety updates
- public information

---

### Nearest Hospitals and Emergency Services

In an emergency, finding help quickly matters.

DisasterWatch BD helps users locate:

- nearby hospitals
- medical centers
- emergency shelters
- rescue facilities
- essential service points

It also shows distance and navigation support to help users reach safety faster.

---

### Smart Shelter Discovery

The platform helps users find certified shelters near their location and view useful shelter details.

Users can check:

- nearest shelters
- shelter location on the map
- available capacity
- overcrowded shelters
- safe navigation routes

---

### Location-Based Emergency Alerts

DisasterWatch BD can detect when a user is near a dangerous or affected zone and provide alerts before the situation becomes worse.

This helps people take action early and avoid unnecessary risk.

---

### Community-Driven Misinformation Protection

False information spreads fast during disasters.
DisasterWatch BD includes a reporting and verification system to help reduce rumors and suspicious claims.

If a piece of information receives enough reports, it can be flagged as unverified until confirmed.

---

## Vision

DisasterWatch BD aims to build a safer, smarter, and more resilient disaster response system for Bangladesh.

The goal is simple: when infrastructure fails, people should still have access to communication, information, and life-saving support.

**DisasterWatch BD keeps communities connected, informed, and prepared when it matters most.**
